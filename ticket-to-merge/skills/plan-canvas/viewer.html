Each card is a file. Arrows follow the request and data flow, left to right. Hover a card to see what the file is for.

Rearrange the plan

Drag a card by its header to move it. Drop it on another card to merge the two files. Drag a function or variable onto another card to move it there, or onto another element to merge the two. Click a card or element to open its drawer, where you can leave a note or delete an element (Esc closes it). Drag the background to pan. Zoom with the buttons, Ctrl or ⌘ and scroll, or a pinch.
